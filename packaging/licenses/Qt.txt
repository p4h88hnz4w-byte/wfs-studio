Qt and PySide6

The window toolkit in a frozen WFS Studio build is PySide6, which is Qt.
PySide6 is available under LGPL-3.0-only, or GPL-2.0-only, or GPL-3.0-only.
The text of LGPL-3.0 and GPL-3.0 is in this folder. GPL-2.0 is published at
https://www.gnu.org/licenses/old-licenses/gpl-2.0.txt

Qt is shipped as shared libraries inside the bundle, next to the other
libraries the build collected. To use your own build of those libraries,
replace the matching files in place and sign the bundle again. The WFS Studio
source stays MIT. That license is the file named LICENSE in this folder.
koolkdev's wfs-tools license is wfs-tools-LICENSE.
